Presented at [DeFi Security Summit 2025](https://defisecuritysummit.org/) in Buenos Aires, Argentina. [AuditHub](https://veridise.com/blog/veridise-announcements/audithub-launches-platform-for-professional-blockchain-audit-firms/) is a platform for professional blockchain security audit firms that provides clients with transparency into the audit process while automating common audit workflows — managing findings, reports, and communications in one place to raise the overall quality bar for blockchain security engagements. This was joint work with [Dr. Kostas Ferles](https://kferles.github.io/) and the Veridise team.

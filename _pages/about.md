Hello! I'm a Staff Compiler Engineer at [Mythic](https://mythic.ai), where I work on compiler infrastructure. 

Over the years I've worked across application security, cryptography (with a focus on [zero-knowledge proofs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zero-knowledge_proof)), distributed systems, program synthesis and analysis, high performance computing, and scientific computing.

Education
=========

I completed an M.S. in Computer Science at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://www.cs.utexas.edu) in December 2022, studying Programming Languages in the [UToPiA group](https://utopia.cs.utexas.edu) under [Dr. Işil Dillig](https://cs.utexas.edu/~isil).
My time at UT was funded by the [Department of Energy Computational Science Graduate Fellowship](https://krellinst.org/csgf) (I was a [2020 fellow](https://www.krellinst.org/csgf/alumni/profile?n=sepanski2020)), where I researched program synthesis, verification, and analysis.
Before that, I finished a B.S. in Mathematics at [Baylor University](https://baylor.edu/math) in May 2020.
